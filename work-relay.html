Relay

Workflow automation for distributed operations teams, designed to remove friction from cross-team handoffs.

Challenge

Relay's operations customers were losing time to manual handoffs between teams, with status updates tracked informally across email and chat rather than inside any system of record.

Approach

We designed a workflow automation layer that makes handoffs explicit. Every task gets an owner, a status and a next step, with automated routing replacing manual coordination.

Scope

Product strategyUX architectureUI designDesign systemsInteractive prototypesDeveloper handoff
The work

Handoffs, made explicit.

Workflow board. Every task visible with its owner and status, replacing informal tracking with a single system of record.

Automated routing rules.

Task detail view.

Team dashboard. A rollup view for managers to see where work is moving quickly, and where it is stalling.

Notification preferences.

Cross-team reporting.

Design thinking
"Automation should feel like relief, not surveillance."

Workflow automation products can easily feel like monitoring tools. We focused the interface on what a task needs next rather than who is behind, framing status as a shared, low-stakes signal rather than a performance metric.

The result is a system operations teams reach for because it removes coordination overhead, not because it's enforced on them.

Outcome
A clearer operational system designed to reduce coordination overhead as Relay's customer teams continue to grow.
